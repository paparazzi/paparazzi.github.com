Detailed Description

INS based in the EKF2 of PX4.

The EKF2 and the PX4 libraries it needs are copied in sw/ext/px4_ekf2 (see sync_px4_ekf2.py there). The GNSS checks, which PX4 runs in its sensors module, are done here before the GNSS data is given to the EKF.
