Detailed Description

Air Data interface.

  • pressures (several barometers and differential pressure sensors)
  • airspeed
  • angle of attack and sideslip
  • wind

◆ air_data_sensor_t

struct air_data_sensor_t

One air data sensor: barometer, differential pressure, airspeed or temperature.

The drivers send the raw values (BARO_ABS_RAW, BARO_DIFF_RAW, AIRSPEED_RAW, TEMPERATURE_RAW) and the calibrated value is (raw - offset) * scale.
