  • airspeed
  • angle of attack and sideslip
  • wind (selected from the estimators and WIND_INFO from the ground)

◆ AIR_DATA_BARO_CALIB

#define AIR_DATA_BARO_CALIB   {}

Calibration of the sensors in the airframe, in a section with prefix AIR_DATA_, for example <define name="DIFF_CALIB" type="array"> <field type="struct"> <field name="abi_id" value="MS45XX_SENDER_ID"> <field name="offset" value="12.3"> <field name="filter_freq" value="2"> </field> </define> The order sets the priority of the automatic selection.

A barometer with calibrated.offset true keeps the offset of the airframe at boot, otherwise it is lined up; a differential pressure always uses the offset of the airframe. A sensor is low-pass filtered with a cut-off of filter_freq (Hz), without filter_freq it isn't filtered.

◆ AIR_DATA_CALIB_AT_BOOT

#define AIR_DATA_CALIB_AT_BOOT   TRUE

Calibrate at boot after AIR_DATA_CALIB_BOOT_DELAY (s): line up the barometers without an offset in the airframe.

The differential pressures are not zeroed at boot (it could be a reboot in flight, or wind): the ones in DIFF_CALIB use the offset of the airframe (0 by default) and are checked at rest, the others are not zeroed until the ZeroAirspeed setting.
